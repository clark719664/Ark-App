FAQ

Common questions

Who owns my domain and website?

You do — full stop. Your domain is registered in your name and stays yours if we ever part ways. We act as the technical manager, and you always have the keys. It’s the industry’s single ugliest dispute, and we designed our service so it can’t happen to you.

I already have a website. Can you take it over?

Yes. Migrations are routine — we document your current setup, move hosting and DNS carefully with zero email downtime, and either keep your existing design or refresh it as part of onboarding.

How fast can my site be live?

Most single-location business sites launch within two weeks of our intake conversation — including domain, email, and Google Business Profile.

What happens if I cancel?

No lock-in and no hostages. You keep your domain, your content, and a full export of your site. We’ll even hand off cleanly to whoever comes next.

Do you work with businesses outside Muskegon?

We do — the lakeshore is home, but everything we manage works anywhere. West Michigan businesses get the option of on-site visits.
