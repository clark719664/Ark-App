💌 Open When letters for the moments that need them

Write the letters they'll need before they need them.

A beloved paper tradition, made sendable: a little bundle of sealed letters — open when you can't sleep, open on the morning of the big day, open when you miss me — delivered as a single link. They get a shelf of wax-sealed envelopes and a reason to think of you on the days that matter.

1 · WritePick the moments, write a few short letters. Prompts help if the page feels blank.
2 · SealYour letters are folded into a single private link. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
3 · SendText it, email it, or tuck it in a card. They open each envelope when its moment comes.

🔒 The letters live inside the link itself — this page has no server, no accounts, and never sees a word you write. It even works offline.
